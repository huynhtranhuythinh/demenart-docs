## 1. Canonical Intent

V128-P5 introduces the **Teacher Assignment semantic** (ngữ nghĩa phân công giáo viên).

The product question is **not** merely:

> “Which teacher account is attached to a class?”

The canonical question is:

> **For a given operational teaching context, which teacher is responsible for teaching, observing, closing out, and/or being presented to the school/parent as the assigned teacher — and who has authority to establish or change that assignment?**

P5 must establish this meaning before any UI or mutation path is built.

---

## 2. Why P5 Exists

The DMA operational surface has evolved through P1–P4:

- **P1 / P2:** weekly school scheduling and related operational capability were proven.
- **P3 — Announcement:** school master/sub-admin gained a controlled writer for school-to-parent in-app announcements.
- **P4 — Home Practice:** teacher closeout gained a durable Home Practice projection attached to `session_reports`, with parent readback and journal continuity.
- **P4 UX correction:** closeout CTA became context-aware when new media is uploaded; deployed state is accepted.

P5 addresses the next semantic gap:

> DMA now has operational sessions and teacher-facing closeout behavior, but the meaning of **“the teacher assigned to this class/session”** must be made explicit and authoritative.

Without that contract, later features can silently conflate:

- account membership,
- school employment,
- class visibility,
- schedule participation,
- session ownership,
- report authoring,
- teacher responsibility,
- substitute coverage,
- and historical attribution.

## 3. Canonical Semantic Distinctions

The following concepts MUST remain distinct unless runtime evidence proves they are intentionally identical.

### 3.1 Teacher Identity (danh tính giáo viên)

A human/teacher profile represented in DMA.

This answers:

> “Who is this teacher?”

It does **not** answer:

> “What are they assigned to?”

---

### 3.2 School Membership / Teacher Access (quyền thành viên / truy cập của giáo viên)

A teacher's relationship to a school or tenant that grants visibility or operational access.

This answers:

> “Can this teacher operate inside this school context?”

It does **not** canonically answer:

> “Are they responsible for this class or session?”

---

### 3.3 Class Assignment (phân công cấp lớp)

A durable responsibility relationship between a teacher and a class/group.

This may represent a primary/default teaching responsibility.

It MUST NOT be inferred solely from:

- who created the class,
- who can view the class,
- who last edited the class,
- who most recently taught a session,
- or a generic teacher-school membership.

---

### 3.4 Session Assignment (phân công cấp buổi học)

The teacher responsible for a specific scheduled lesson session.

This may:

- inherit from class assignment,
- explicitly override class assignment,
- represent substitute coverage,
- or be historically frozen after the session occurs.

The exact rule is a P5 discovery/decision target and is **not yet assumed**.

---

### 3.5 Session Actor / Report Author (người thao tác / tác giả báo cáo)

The authenticated user who creates, updates, closes out, or submits data.

This is an **audit fact**.

It MUST NOT automatically redefine the assigned teacher.

Example:

- Teacher A is assigned to the session.
- Admin B corrects a scheduling field.
- Teacher C submits a report under an authorized substitution workflow.

The actor and the assigned teacher may differ.

---

### 3.6 Display Teacher (giáo viên hiển thị)

The teacher name/avatar presented to parents, school staff, or records.

Display projection MUST be derived from a defined semantic source.

It MUST NOT be chosen opportunistically from whichever teacher-related field happens to be populated.

---

## 4. Core P5 Invariants

### INV-P5-01 — Assignment ≠ Access

**Teacher Assignment (phân công giáo viên) is not equivalent to Teacher Access (quyền truy cập của giáo viên).**

A teacher may have school/class access without being assigned responsibility.

---

### INV-P5-02 — Assignment ≠ Actor

The authenticated actor performing a mutation is not automatically the assigned teacher.

Audit attribution and teaching responsibility are separate facts.

---

### INV-P5-03 — No Historical Rewrite by Default

A change in current class assignment MUST NOT silently rewrite historical teacher attribution for already-completed sessions.

Historical session truth must remain stable unless a deliberate correction workflow exists.

---

### INV-P5-04 — Substitution Must Be Representable

The model must be capable of representing a session taught by a substitute/cover teacher without corrupting the durable class assignment.

---

### INV-P5-05 — Authority Is Explicit

The system must define who may:

- establish a class assignment,
- change a class assignment,
- override a session assignment,
- remove an assignment,
- and correct historical assignment.

No UI control should be built before the corresponding authority rule is proven.

---

### INV-P5-06 — Read Projection Is Deterministic

Any surface showing “Teacher / Giáo viên” must have a deterministic source-of-truth rule.

No component-local fallback chain may become de facto product semantics.

---

### INV-P5-07 — Unknown Is Allowed

If an assignment is unknown/unset, the system should represent that state explicitly rather than fabricating a teacher from unrelated metadata.

**Unknown ≠ invalid.**

---

## 5. Required P5 Semantic Questions

P5.1 must answer, from runtime truth and Owner decisions where needed:

1. What teacher-related fields/tables/RPCs already exist?
2. Is there currently a class-level teacher relationship?
3. Is there currently a session-level teacher relationship?
4. Which field is used by `/school/schedule`?
5. Which field is used by teacher class/session views?
6. Which teacher is shown in parent-facing outcomes/journal surfaces, if any?
7. Can multiple teachers be associated with one class?
8. Can one session override the class teacher?
9. What happens when a teacher changes mid-term?
10. What happens to past sessions after reassignment?
11. Who currently has mutation authority?
12. Does the existing authority match the intended product model?
13. Is substitution already representable?
14. Is assignment required or nullable?
15. What audit evidence exists for assignment changes?

---

## 6. Explicit Non-Assumptions

Until P5.1 proves otherwise, DO NOT assume:

- `teacher_id` means “assigned teacher” without examining its contract.
- the teacher returned by `get_teacher_classes` is the authoritative assigned teacher.
- the creator of `lesson_session` is the teacher.
- the teacher submitting `session_report` is the scheduled teacher.
- a class has exactly one teacher.
- a session must always inherit a class teacher.
- admins and teachers have the same assignment mutation authority.
- reassignment should retroactively affect history.
- parent-facing teacher attribution already has correct semantics.

---

## 7. Product-Level Target State

A successful P5 capability should eventually allow DMA to answer, without ambiguity:

> “Who is responsible for this class?”

> “Who is teaching this specific session?”

> “Was this session covered by someone else?”

> “Who changed the assignment, when, and under what authority?”

> “Which teacher should be shown to the parent for this completed learning event?”

The answer may use one field or several, but the semantic contract must be explicit.

---

## 8. Canonical Layering

Recommended conceptual layering:

1. **Teacher Identity** (danh tính)
2. **School Membership / Access** (quyền tham gia)
3. **Class Assignment** (phân công mặc định)
4. **Session Assignment / Override** (phân công buổi học / ghi đè)
5. **Operational Actor** (người thao tác)
6. **Historical Attribution** (ghi nhận lịch sử)
7. **Display Projection** (phép chiếu hiển thị)
8. **Audit Trail** (dấu vết kiểm toán)

P5 discovery must determine which of these already exist and where new substrate is genuinely required.

---

## 9. Build Gate

**P5.0 does not authorize implementation.**

Build may begin only after:

- runtime schema/RPC/frontend usage is audited;
- existing teacher semantics are mapped;
- ambiguity is classified;
- Owner decisions are captured for unresolved product choices;
- mutation authority is explicit;
- historical behavior is explicit;
- scope is bounded.

---

## 10. Canonical Decision

**V128-P5.0 ACCEPTED SEMANTIC:**

> Teacher Assignment is an operational responsibility relationship, not merely an account/access relationship. Class-level responsibility, session-level responsibility, mutation actor, historical attribution, and display projection must be distinguished until proven equivalent by the existing system contract.

This semantic is the governing basis for V128-P5.1 and all later P5 implementation work.
