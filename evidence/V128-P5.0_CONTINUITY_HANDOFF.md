## 2. Why the Next Capability Is Teacher Assignment

P1–P4 have progressively made DMA more operational:

- school schedule exists;
- lesson sessions exist;
- teachers can execute closeout workflows;
- parents receive durable learning outcomes;
- school operators can send communications.

The next risk is **semantic ambiguity around which teacher owns responsibility for a class/session**.

Before expanding workflows, DMA needs an explicit assignment contract so that future capabilities do not accidentally bind business semantics to incidental implementation facts.

## 4. Semantic Target

P5 investigates and defines:

### A. Class Assignment
Who is the default/responsible teacher for a class?

### B. Session Assignment
Who is responsible for a specific lesson session?

### C. Override/Substitution
Can a session use a different teacher from the class default?

### D. Authority
Who may establish/change/clear these assignments?

### E. Historical Attribution
What teacher identity remains attached to completed sessions after later reassignment?

### F. Projection
Which teacher is displayed to school staff, teachers, and parents?

### G. Audit
How is assignment creation/change recorded?
